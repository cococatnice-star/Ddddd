---
name: CocoX.wtf vouch policy
description: Product decision for public feedback cards in the CocoX.wtf Discord bot.
---

Only publish a vouch card when the Discord member named on it submits their own feedback. Do not invent usernames or endorsements, and do not post vouches on a timer. Clearly label any demo content as a sample.

**Why:** The user accepted a safer alternative to randomly named vouches posted every six seconds.

**How to apply:** Keep public vouch posts tied to a deliberate submission by the attributed Discord account. If adding examples or demos, mark them clearly as samples.
